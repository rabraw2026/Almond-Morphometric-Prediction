RUN 3 — MULTIPLE LINEAR REGRESSION: HULL THICKNESS

This package contains the supplementary material for the third multiple linear regression experiment, in which fruit morphometric ratios (L/W, W/T, and L/T) were used to predict 12 hull-thickness measurements.

CAPTIONS

Supplementary Figure S3. Observed versus predicted values for the 12 hull-thickness response variables obtained using multiple linear regression. Panels are arranged by measurement side and longitudinal position: (a–c) Rr top, middle, and bottom; (d–f) Rl top, middle, and bottom; (g–i) lr top, middle, and bottom; and (j–l) Ll top, middle, and bottom. The diagonal line represents perfect agreement between observed and predicted values.

Supplementary Figure S4. Residual plots for the 12 hull-thickness response variables obtained using multiple linear regression. Panels are arranged by measurement side and longitudinal position: (a–c) Rr top, middle, and bottom; (d–f) Rl top, middle, and bottom; (g–i) lr top, middle, and bottom; and (j–l) Ll top, middle, and bottom. The horizontal line at zero represents zero prediction error.

Supplementary Figure S5. Regression coefficients for the three fruit morphometric predictors (L/W, W/T, and L/T) in the 12 hull-thickness multiple linear regression models. Panels are arranged by measurement side and longitudinal position: (a–c) Rr top, middle, and bottom; (d–f) Rl top, middle, and bottom; (g–i) lr top, middle, and bottom; and (j–l) Ll top, middle, and bottom. Positive and negative coefficients indicate the direction of the fitted linear association with each hull-thickness response.

IMPORTANT TERMINOLOGY NOTE
The original individual plots were titled “Feature Importance,” but their x-axis is “Coefficient Value” and they contain signed positive and negative values. Therefore, in the submission package the combined figure is correctly named “MLR_Coefficients” rather than “Feature Importance.”
